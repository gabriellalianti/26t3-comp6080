## Make a box with CSS properties
### Task1
In `box.html` can you make a box that is:
 * `100px` wide
 * `50px` high
 * `3px` of padding
 * `1px` border that is solid and of colour `#333333`
 * `5px` of margin on the left and right, and `10px` of margin on the top and bottom?
 * background colour of `rgb(255,255,0)`

### Task2
In `box.html`, add a second `<div>` with class `box2` and style it so that it is:
 * `200px` wide and `100px` high
 * Padding of `5px` on the top, `10px` on the right, `15px` on the bottom, and `20px` on the left (use the 4-value shorthand)
 * Top and bottom borders that are `2px`, `solid`, and coloured `#FF0000`
 * Left and right borders that are `4px`, `dashed`, and coloured `#0000FF`
 * A border radius of `10px` on all corners
 * Background colour of `rgba(0, 128, 255, 0.3)`
 * `20px` of margin on all sides
